June 2022 Katowice, PL
Co-organiser Workshop

Resilience for All — UN-Habitat World Urban Forum

An interdisciplinary training event hosted by TU Delft

A 3-hour workshop bringing scientific planning support tools and methods to urban planners, policymakers, and civil servants — across socio-spatial equity, geographic data science, participatory co-design, and spatial tools for resilience. Co-organised with Dr. Nazli Yonca Aydin, supported by four TU Delft thematic labs.

Speakers Roberto Rocco · Trivik Verma · Carissa Champlin · Claudiu Forgaci · Juliana Goncalves · Ylenia Casali · Mikhail Sirenko
Supported by TPM Resilience Lab · TPM AI Lab · CUSP · TU Delft Global Urban Lab

2017 San Francisco
Organiser · Exhibitor Exhibition · Seminar

Adaptation by Design — Delta Interventions Studio

Synthesis of the TU Delft Delta Urbanism Research Programme, 2016–2017

Exhibition and seminar reflecting on the Delta Interventions Studio's work in San Francisco Bay — a precursor to the BCDC's Resilience by Design competition. In collaboration with UC Berkeley's College of Environmental Design, the studio addressed Bay Area's adaptive capacity to climate uncertainty through systems thinking, landscape dynamics, urban morphology, and adaptation pathways.

Leaders Han Meyer · Taneha Bacchin
Themes Systems thinking · Building with Nature · Adaptive reuse · Cultural heritage

2021 Amsterdam, NL
Lead Expert Consultation

RE-GROUND — Resilient land-use scenarios for the Metropolitan Region of Amsterdam

Strategic consultation with planning practitioners and policy experts

A methodology that combines model-based urban land-use simulations with design thinking to develop resilient spatial planning scenarios. Run as an exploratory expert design workshop with practitioners to align computational findings with local planning realities — testbed for spatial evidence on long-term urbanisation strategies.

Linked to The Future Ground (PhD)
2018 Tokyo, JP
Exhibitor Exhibition

22 Challenges in Flooding — Gallery TEN, Tokyo

Exhibition on collaborative design-engineering for Edogawa ward

Exhibited the Edogawa Water City and Parkway Edogawa design alternatives — proposals combining hydraulic engineering with spatial planning to reduce flood risk in one of Tokyo's most vulnerable wards. The exhibition framed the work alongside other contemporary responses to flooding in dense coastal regions.
